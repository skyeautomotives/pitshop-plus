# Pitshop Plus

Billing and service management for automobile workshops, by Skye Automotives.

**Website:** https://pitshopplus.com/

## What it does

- **Bills:** parts and work items, labour, painting and washing, discounts. Print, save as PDF or send on WhatsApp. Record what the customer pays after billing (cash, UPI and more, in one go or in parts); the bill shows the advance, payments and the balance still to pay.
- **Job cards:** tick the common jobs for bikes or cars (or write your own) when a vehicle comes in, and link the bills that fix it. Note what came in with the vehicle (vehicle name, km driven, helmets, RC book, pollution certificate, licence, insurance and anything else) and any advance payment, which the bill then shows as already paid.
- **Find and history:** search by customer name, phone or vehicle number, and see each vehicle's complaints and bills in one place.
- **Reports:** daily and monthly sales.
- **Staff logins:** the owner adds logins for the team.
- **Works offline:** keep billing without internet; changes are sent when you're back online.
- **Backup & restore:** save a copy of your records to your own Google Drive or computer.

## Get it

Register your workshop on the website, then use it in the browser or download the Windows or Android app from the **Download** page. One account works on all your devices.

## Using it

- **Settings:** enter the workshop name, address and phone printed on your bills, and add staff logins.
- **New bill:** customer name, phone, vehicle number, date received and labour charge are required.
- **Backups:** save a backup at least once a week from **Settings, Backup & restore**, and keep it in your Google Drive. See the [Terms & Conditions](https://pitshopplus.com/terms).
